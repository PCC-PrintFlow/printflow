Creators

Business Information

Invoicing Defaults

Invoice numbers are generated as INV-<Company Code>-<Year>-<Unique Number>, e.g. INV-PCC-2026-1001. You can still type over the suggested number on any invoice.

Costs & Margin

Machine cost covers electricity, wear parts and printer upkeep for each hour of printing. Labor is what your hands-on time is worth (cleanup, assembly, packing). Products and invoices below the target margin are flagged; leave it at 0 to turn the flags off. Margin = (price − cost) ÷ price. Products marked "Makes purge blocks" get their share of the purge block price taken off their cost.

Craft Fairs

Craft fair prices include sales tax; the Craft Fairs section backs it out of each sale. Indiana charges a flat 7% state sales tax with no county or city add-on. Venmo fees apply to Venmo sales only (cash has no fee). Past fairs keep the rates they were closed out with.

Order Form Defaults

Order numbers are generated as ORD-<Company Code>-<Year>-<Number> (using the same Company Code above), e.g. ORD-PCC-2026-0101. The Need By date auto-fills this many days from the order date.

Data

All data (customers, catalog, invoices, settings) is stored locally in this browser. Export a backup periodically, especially before clearing browser data.

Sync between devices

Keep your PC and iPad up to date: Push sends this device's data to your private GitHub repository; on the other device, Pull brings it down and replaces that device's data. Push when you finish on one device, Pull before you start on the other.

Sync works in the installed PrintFlow app (Claude's viewer can't connect to GitHub). Here you can still use Export / Import Backup above.

The token and repository name are stored only on this device and are never included in pushes or backups. Use a private repository.
